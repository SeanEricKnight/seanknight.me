About

The brief version

I’ve spent most of my career where technology, customers, operations, and leadership collide. I started with hands-on technical work, built and ran small businesses, led technical teams, managed enterprise healthcare implementations and strategic accounts, and later moved into information security governance.

The titles changed. The underlying work did not: find the real problem, help people understand one another, and keep responsibility visible until something useful happens.

I live in Spring Hill, Tennessee, near Nashville.
